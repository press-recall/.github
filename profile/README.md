# Recall 🧠

**Think fast. Remember more. Know when to press.**

Recall is a fast-paced memory and reaction game built around one simple decision:

> **Press — or don't press.**

The rules may be simple, but as the game progresses, remembering previous patterns, staying focused, and making the right decision under pressure becomes increasingly challenging.

## 🎮 About Recall

Recall is designed to test several cognitive skills through simple, interactive gameplay:

* 🧠 **Memory** — Remember patterns, sequences, and previous events.
* 🎯 **Focus** — Stay concentrated as the game becomes more demanding.
* ⚡ **Reaction** — Make decisions quickly when the moment comes.
* 🤔 **Decision-making** — Know when to act and, more importantly, when not to.
* 📈 **Progressive difficulty** — The challenge increases as you improve.

The core idea is intentionally minimal: **give the player a simple rule and make remembering that rule harder and harder.**

## 🕹️ How It Works

Each round presents you with a situation where you have to decide whether to press the button.

Sometimes you should press.

Sometimes you shouldn't.

Your job is to remember what you've seen, understand the current situation, and make the correct choice before it's too late.

A single mistake can change the outcome.

**How long can you keep your focus?**

## 🚀 Our Goal

Recall is more than a small browser game. It's an experiment in creating engaging gameplay from simple mechanics.

We're exploring how minimal interactions can create meaningful challenges through:

* Game mechanics
* Cognitive challenges
* User experience and interface design
* Difficulty progression
* Performance and responsiveness
* Modern web technologies

The goal is to keep the experience **simple to understand, difficult to master, and satisfying to play.**
